ElementsKit is an ultimate all-in-one addons pack for the Elementor page builder. It brings a comprehensive solution with 90+ Elementor widgets, 18+ modules, and 900+ pre-designed Elementor templates. Everything is accessible through an easy drag-and-drop interface, including features like a header and footer builder.

## Highlights

- 🧩 90+ Elementor widgets and 18+ modules
- 🎨 900+ pre-designed templates
- 📐 Header and footer builder
- 🖱️ Drag-and-drop throughout, no custom CSS required
- ⚡ Performance work aimed at keeping large sites fast

## Role

Led development of ElementsKit at Roxnor and scaled it from 700K to nearly 2M active installations through sustained feature enhancement and performance optimization.
