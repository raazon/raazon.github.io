Forminator is not your typical form-making plugin. It is the easy-to-use WordPress form builder for every website and situation, covering contact forms, order forms, payment forms, and email forms.

## Highlights

- 💬 Contact, order, payment, and email forms
- 📊 Feedback widgets, interactive polls with real-time results, and quizzes
- 🧾 Service estimators and registration forms with payment options
- 💳 PayPal and Stripe payment integration
- 🛠️ Drag-and-drop builder with no code required
