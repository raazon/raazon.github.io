Linked Variation for WooCommerce is an open-source plugin I built to solve a recurring WooCommerce problem: managing large sets of product variations that share the same attributes.

Instead of editing every variation by hand, the plugin links products based on the attributes you select, so a change to one option propagates across the linked set.

## Highlights

- 🔗 Link products automatically based on shared attributes
- 🔄 Simplifies maintenance of complex variable product catalogs
- 🧩 Works alongside WooCommerce's native variation handling
- 📦 Released on WordPress.org under an open license

## Problem it solves

Storefronts with colour and size matrices can end up with hundreds of manual variation records. Linked Variation keeps them in sync from one place, which cuts down on mistakes and maintenance time.
